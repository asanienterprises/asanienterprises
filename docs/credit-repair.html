FCRA

Fair Credit Reporting Act

The Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) is the federal law that regulates how consumer credit information is collected, reported, and disputed.

FCRA

Right to accurate reporting

Consumers have the right to ensure that information reported by credit bureaus such as Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion is accurate and verifiable.

FCRA

Right to dispute information

The FCRA allows consumers to dispute inaccurate, outdated, or unverifiable information appearing on their credit reports.

FCRA

Investigation requirements

Credit reporting agencies must investigate disputes submitted by consumers and correct or remove information that cannot be verified.
