FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Common questions related to credit consulting and credit repair.

What services does Asani Enterprises provide?

Asani Enterprises provides credit consulting, strategic credit repair guidance, credit profile review, and business credit guidance.

How do I get started?

You can begin by completing the secure client portal and submitting your information for review.

Is my information private?

Information submitted through the secure portal is handled confidentially and used only in connection with the services provided.

Can you guarantee results?

No. Outcomes depend on individual circumstances, supporting documentation, creditor responses, and credit reporting agency procedures.

What does credit repair involve?

Credit repair consulting may involve reviewing reporting accuracy, identifying disputed items, organizing documentation, and recommending next steps based on the profile reviewed.

Can you help with inaccurate items on my credit report?

Yes. Consulting may include evaluating inaccurate, outdated, or unverifiable reporting and identifying the most appropriate way to address those items.

What documents may be needed?

Documents may include identification, proof of address, credit-related records, and any supporting documents relevant to the review of your profile.

How long does credit repair take?

Timing varies based on the issues being reviewed, the documentation available, and the response times of creditors and credit reporting agencies.

Do you work with business owners?

Yes. Asani Enterprises also provides guidance for entrepreneurs seeking stronger business credit positioning and improved financing readiness.

Will checking my credit hurt my score?

A review of the documents and information you provide through the portal does not itself create a hard inquiry. Any separate lender or creditor inquiry would depend on that institution’s process.
